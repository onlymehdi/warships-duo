Warships Duo Support

Warships Duo is an offline pass-and-play naval game for iPhone and iPad.

Contact

Questions, bug reports or feedback: onlymehdi@gmail.com. Please include your device model and iOS version.

Common questions

Can I play without a second person? Yes. Choose Play the Computer at Easy, Normal or Hard.

How do two players share one device? A cover screen hides both boards between turns. Hand the device over and tap "I'm ..." to continue.

Does the app need an internet connection or an account? No. It works fully offline and collects no data.

Do I have to shake or flip the device? No. Every gesture also has a button.
