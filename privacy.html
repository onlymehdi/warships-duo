Warships Duo Privacy Policy

Effective 16 November 2026

Summary

Warships Duo does not collect, store off your device, share or sell any personal data. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it never connects to the internet.

What stays on your device

The match in progress, the two captain names you choose, your achievements and career record, and your settings (theme and sound). This is saved only in the app's own storage on your device.

What is collected

Nothing. The app sends no data to the developer or to anyone else.

Motion

If you turn on Flip to pass or shake to roll the dice, the app reads the device's motion sensors while that screen is open. The readings are used at that moment and are never saved or sent anywhere.

Children

The app is rated 9+ and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes everything it has saved. You can also clear a match from the match menu.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will ship with an app update. For questions, email onlymehdi@gmail.com.
